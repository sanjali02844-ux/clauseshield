Potentially relevant legal provisions to verify for your state and situation:

Model Framework

Model Tenancy Act, 2021

Provides guidelines for renting houses and settling problems between landlords and tenants. It covers tenancy agreements, security deposit caps (typically up to two months' rent for residential premises), and return of deposits upon handover.

Contract Law

Indian Contract Act, 1872 – Section 74

When you sign a rental agreement, both you and your landlord are expected to follow its terms. If the agreement mentions a penalty or forfeiture, the landlord cannot automatically forfeit any arbitrary amount without demonstrating reasonable compensation for actual loss.

Consumer Protection

Consumer Protection Act, 2019

Protects consumers against unfair trade practices and deficiency in service. In managed rentals, student housing, PG accommodations, or platform-based leasing services, tenants can seek redressal for arbitrary withholdings.

Important Note

These laws help you understand your rights, but the final outcome depends on your rental agreement, evidence, and the tenancy rules applicable in your state. For serious disputes, consult a qualified lawyer.
