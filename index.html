ABOUT CLAUSESHIELD

Make rental information easier to understand.

ClauseShield is a student prototype designed to help tenants organise rental concerns, understand questions to ask, and prepare clearer communications.

What it aims to do

Reduce information gaps by turning dense rental wording into plain-language prompts and practical next steps.

Who it is for

Students, first-time renters, working professionals and other tenants who want to keep their rental records organised.

Responsible use

Legal outcomes depend on the agreement, evidence, state rules and individual facts. Seek professional advice for legal decisions.
